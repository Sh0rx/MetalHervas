The camera hovers high above the valley at blue hour, then smoothly descends while turning 180 degrees in a calm arc to face back toward the house it came from. It settles at first-floor height on a straight, frontal view of the two-leaf anthracite grey window in the cream travertine stone facade, both sashes open inward, warm light in the empty room behind. The window keeps its exact shape, colour and sashes. One continuous shot, no cuts, no camera shake, no people, no text. Photorealistic.
