The camera floats in the evening air in front of the house, looking out over the valley, and slowly turns 180 degrees in a smooth, calm arc to face back toward the house it came from. It settles on a straight, frontal view of the two-leaf anthracite grey window in the cream travertine stone facade, both sashes open inward, warm light in the room behind. The window keeps its exact shape, colour and sashes at all times. Blue hour. One continuous shot, no cuts, no camera shake, no people, no text. Photorealistic.
