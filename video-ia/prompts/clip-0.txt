Slow, steady dolly forward through the window workshop toward the nearest white window standing on the floor. As the camera moves closer, the whole image gradually and smoothly goes out of focus, and cool blue-hour light with a soft mountain silhouette fades in from the right, blending with the warm workshop light on the left. The windows never change shape, colour, number or position while they are still sharp. One continuous shot, no cuts, no camera shake, no people, no text. Photorealistic.
